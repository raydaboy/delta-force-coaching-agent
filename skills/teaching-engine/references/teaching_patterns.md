# Teaching patterns

Use these patterns as writing shapes, not as facts. Replace every bracketed item with evidence from the current recording.

## Loss after a bad first trade

**Observed:** “You took damage while [visible exposure].” **Inference:** “That likely removed the safe path to finish.” **Alternative:** “Break to [visible reset point] before re-peeking.” **Trade-off:** “You give up the angle and a chance to finish immediately.” **Goal consequence:** “That preserves a better chance to heal and keep the loot route.” **Cue:** “Bad first trade: break the line.” **Drill:** On the next three fights, name the reset before the first shot.

## Win from a prepared advantage

**Observed:** “You had [audio/height/surprise/cover] before the target appeared.” **Inference:** “That likely gave your aim time to work.” **Risk:** “The same push would be less safe if [visible condition] changed.” **Alternative:** “Keep [specific advantage] for the next target instead of spending it on [risk].” **Cue:** “Keep the advantage for the second target.” **Drill:** After every down, change the angle before exposing again.

## Loot interruption

**Observed:** “The loot interface was open when [damage/shot/nearby enemy] appeared.” **Decision:** “Cancel the interaction and move to [visible cover].” **Trade-off:** “The item waits, but you lose less health and keep control.” **Cue:** “Loot can wait; cover and aim cannot.” **Drill:** Exit the loot screen within one second of a damage cue in three consecutive fights.

## Multi-target cluster

**Observed:** “Several targets pressure [connected doorway/lane/stairs].” **Inference:** “Keeping the same lane lets more than one enemy act.” **Alternative:** “Use [smoke/height/choke/landing] to make the next target enter alone.” **Trade-off:** “The finish may take longer.” **Cue:** “Make the next angle smaller.” **Drill:** Call target one, reset, then expose only after the first threat is solved.

## Extraction-route threat

**Observed:** “The enemy is close to [route/extraction/loot position] and contact is forced.” **Alternative:** “Take the fast safe exchange from [visible cover], then keep moving.” **Trade-off:** “Do not stop to browse or chase information.” **Cue:** “Win the route, not the ego fight.” **Drill:** After a route fight, heal or reload only once, then move to the next safe landmark.

## Repeated principle with changed context

Name the old principle and the new condition. Example: “The earlier lesson was to break line after a bad trade. Here, the fight is different: you have smoke and a split squad, so the better version is to change cover after each target rather than disengage completely.” Label this `progression` or `different_version`; never present it as a new lesson.

## Unknowns

Use “we can see,” “the frame shows,” and “the audio confirms” for observed evidence. Use “likely,” “could,” and “may” for inferences. Use “we cannot confirm” for unknowns. Never state that an unseen teammate exists, that an enemy intended a specific action, or that an alternative would definitely win.
