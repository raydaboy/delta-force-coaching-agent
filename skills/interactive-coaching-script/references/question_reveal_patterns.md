# Question-and-Reveal Patterns

## Position and cover

**Question:** “Before you heal, which angle is actually blocked, and which angle is still open?”

**Reveal:** Name the visible barrier, the visible gap, and the observed consequence. If the shooter is off-screen, label the exact position unknown.

## Utility timing

**Question:** “Do you spend the grenade on uncertain movement, or hold it for the route that becomes predictable?”

**Reveal:** Show whether the target location or health was confirmed, then show the later route or resource need. Explain opportunity cost, not certainty.

## Advantage conversion

**Question:** “You have the first down. What makes the next action safe enough to continue?”

**Reveal:** Identify whether the player created isolation, hard cover, reload time, or information. A kill alone does not prove that the follow-up was correct.

## Disengagement

**Question:** “What would you give up by retreating now, and what risk do you remove?”

**Reveal:** Show the observed injury, exposure, objective pressure, or reset opportunity. State the trade-off of disengaging.

## Target priority

**Question:** “Which visible target changes the fight most if left alive?”

**Reveal:** Use visible threat, weapon, angle, and proximity. Do not infer hidden equipment or intent.

## Enemy-view framing

**Question:** “From the visible geometry, what can the opponent plausibly see?”

**Reveal:** Draw only supported sightlines and cover gaps. Label the panel `INFERRED FROM VISIBLE GEOMETRY` and list what remains unknown.
