# Interactive Coaching Episode Workflow

## 1. Episode promise

Open with a real action and consequence, then state the scope of the review. The first spoken agenda should name strengths worth preserving and weaknesses that the full session actually supports. Limit the promise to two or three objectives.

A useful opening pattern is:

> “After reviewing this session, I found that you consistently create first advantages with [strength]. The biggest costs come from [priority one] and [priority two]. Today we will see the evidence, test the decisions, and finish with drills.”

## 2. Question-and-reveal beats

A question beat should occur before the analysis is revealed. The scene must show enough evidence for the player to reason about the choice. Use a short pause; do not ask a question whose answer depends on hidden enemy information.

Recommended sequence:

1. Show the live setup.
2. Pause on the decision frame.
3. Ask one concrete question.
4. Leave a thinking window.
5. Reveal what was observable.
6. Explain the decision and immediate effect.
7. Show the consequence.
8. State the likely alternative and trade-off.

## 3. Pattern bridges

After a deep lesson, connect it to another timestamp with a short bridge:

- “This is the same reset problem we saw earlier, but here you had a different weapon and a different resource constraint.”
- “The pattern improved here: you held the angle after the first down instead of re-peeking immediately.”
- “This next fight is not the same mistake. The player state was different, so the recommendation changes.”

Pattern bridges must cite the linked fight IDs and avoid treating different contexts as identical.

## 4. Progress checks

Use later-session moments to test whether a behavior repeated, improved, or remained uncertain. A single later success does not prove mastery. Describe the evidence:

- `improved_once`
- `repeated`
- `mixed`
- `not_observable`

## 5. Script quality control

Before passing the episode to video production, check that the cold open has a consequence, the agenda is supported, questions precede reveals, every claim has a class, alternatives have trade-offs, wins explain advantage conversion, losses preserve correct decisions, and drills are measurable.
